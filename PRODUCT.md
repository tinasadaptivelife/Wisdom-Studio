# Wisdom Studio

## What it is

A free, open-source, Canva-like design app for creating print-ready PDFs — coloring pages, flyers, storybook pages, and lyric/script sheets — with built-in AI image generation (AI Horde). Outputs are given away, not sold.

## Register

product — app UI where design serves the task. The tool should disappear into the work of making a page.

## Platform

web

## Audience

Adults 50+, including users with low vision, tremor, or limited tech confidence. Many are creative hobbyists (storytellers, community organizers, grandparents making things for grandkids). Accessibility is a hard requirement, not polish: large targets, high contrast, plain language, keyboard operability, no time pressure.

## Brand voice

Warm, encouraging, unhurried. Plain language over jargon ("Bring to front", never "z-index"). The app is a craft table, not a cockpit — colorful and friendly, but never cluttered or noisy. A small number of big, clearly labeled actions beats dense toolbars.

## Design principles

1. **Legibility first.** Type, contrast, and spacing tuned for older eyes. Nothing below 16px body. WCAG 2.1 AA is the floor.
2. **Warm, not loud.** A warm cream canvas, one confident accent for primary actions, a deep navy for structure. Color signals state and identity, never decoration for its own sake.
3. **Earned familiarity.** Standard patterns (top bar, side tools, properties panel) done exactly right — no invented affordances.
4. **Big friendly targets.** 44×44px minimum everywhere; generous padding; obvious focus.
5. **Patience built in.** Long AI generation waits get clear, honest progress — nothing disappears or times out on the user.

## Anti-references

- Dense pro tools (Photoshop, Blender) — intimidating for this audience.
- Generic AI-slop dashboards — purple gradients, glassmorphism, tiny gray text.
- Toy-like oversimplification — users are adults; warm ≠ childish.
